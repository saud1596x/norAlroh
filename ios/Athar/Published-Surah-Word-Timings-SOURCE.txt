native Mushaf word IDs and ayah numbers; verified publisher redirects against
all114 exact application chapter URLs for each imported recording. A pinned

coordinates cover all77429 native word IDs; Shuraim's Fatiha basmala has no

coverage, not an independent acoustic review or listening-quality approval.
