Changes by Noor Alruh: bound the original published word coordinates to the
approved native Mushaf IDs using the existing exact coordinate bridge. Original
integer timestamps, repeats and gaps are preserved. No inferred/interpolated
clock spans or substitute recordings. All114 published audio URLs per recording
were checked by HEAD against their exact application URL or public redirect.
IDs1000001/1000002/1000003 are Noor-internal recording IDs, not provider IDs.

Each imported recording covers all77429 native words and6236 ayahs, including
the first basmala. This is coordinate coverage, not independent acoustic review,
listening-quality approval or learner assessment. The publisher uses automated
alignment with community validation; the word-tier files provide no per-word
confidence or independent human-review flags.

Recitation recordings remain the property of their reciters and original sources.
No audio is redistributed here; playback uses the exact public source recording.
Package/source/native hashes and identity observations are preserved in
content-sources/reciter-timings/additional-recordings-receipt.json and
additional-audio-identity-proof.json.
